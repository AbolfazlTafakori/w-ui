# Support W-UI

W-UI is built and released for free, in the open, by one developer. Every feature, every fix, the documentation in two languages and the CI that installs it on ten Linux distributions before each release are done on personal time and paid for out of pocket — the test servers, the build machines, the hours.

We want to keep going: the W-UI app, more protocols, and a panel that stays ahead of every block put in your customers' way. Whether that happens depends on the people who use it. If W-UI runs your business, or keeps your own connection open, it is yours to keep alive as much as ours. A donation of any size is what decides whether the next release comes.
